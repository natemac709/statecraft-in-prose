# Corpus Methods & Reference Frameworks

## Free Corpus Analysis Tools
• AntConc (laurenceanthony.net/software/antconc): Free concordancer for checking keyword-in-context (KWIC) lines, collocations, and modal verb frequency ("shall" vs. "will" vs. "supports").

• Python (`spaCy` / `NLTK`): Optional lightweight scripts for measuring lexical density, passive-voice ratios, and word overlap between prompt and reply pairs.

## Related Literature & Anchors
• Forensic Linguistics & Contract Readability: Peter Tiersma (*Legal Language*) and empirical studies on syntactic complexity in legal and procurement texts.

• Bureaucratese & Plain Language: Research on public administration prose alongside the Federal Plain Language Guidelines (plainlanguage.gov) and the Plain Writing Act of 2010.
