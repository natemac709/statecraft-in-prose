# Sampling Scope & Limitations

1. Contested Record Bias: Publicly available evaluation quotes come largely from protested procurements (GAO/COFC) or FOIA-released decision documents, which skew toward high-dollar or disputed awards rather than routine unprotested contracts.
2. Written Text Only: Analysis covers only the published written record and does not capture oral presentations, pre-solicitation industry days, or internal agency deliberations.
3. Descriptive, Not Legal: Grammar notes describe linguistic structure and clarity, not legal enforceability under the FAR.
4. Third-Party Data in Public Records: Official `.gov` records sometimes include data or text supplied by third parties (such as Dun & Bradstreet fields in older SAM.gov records), which may carry separate attribution, reuse, or non-bulk limits.
