# Case 002: Federal Program Manager Threshold & "General Terms" (`B-421227.2; B-421227.3`)

- **Docket:** https://www.gao.gov/products/b-421227.2%2Cb-421227.3 (Solicitation No. `2021-20111300002S`)
- **Agency:** Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI)
- **Reviewer Verdict:** Sustained (Protest sustained Aug. 26, 2024, including challenge to evaluation of proposed program manager)

## 0. Stated Purpose

> The RFP sought proposals to provide a range of advisory services and support to improve the processes and capabilities surrounding the development, defense, execution, reporting, and process analysis of activities required to develop the NIP submission to the Office of Management and Budget and Congress.

## 1. The Prompt

> The SOW defined the qualifications for the program manager, including, as relevant here, “Project Management Professional (PMP) or equivalent PM certification and minimum of seven (7) years relevant experience as a Program Manager within the Federal Government.”

## 2. The Reply

> In a narrative addressing the required qualifications, according to Guidehouse’s proposal, X “has over 8 years’ experience managing programs with varying degrees of complexity within the Federal Government.”

## 3. The Reviewer's Note

> Under Section 2 of the proposed candidate’s resume, the Offeror stated in general terms that the candidate has “over eight years of experience managing programs with varying degrees of complexity within the Federal Government.”

## Plain-English Gloss

ODNI's solicitation for intelligence-program budget and process support required a program manager with a PMP (or equivalent) and at least seven years of relevant experience as a federal program manager. Guidehouse's narrative stated that its proposed candidate had over eight years of experience managing programs of varying complexity across the federal government, and the evaluators called out that claim as stated in general terms.

- **Later Contract Record:** Protest `B-421227.2; B-421227.3` sustained August 26, 2024 (following initial award to KPMG LLP at `$53,027,362` versus Guidehouse at `$29,382,335` under Solicitation `2021-20111300002S`).

## Nuance Lost in Translation

A summary that says "the vendor claimed eight years of experience and the evaluator pushed back" misses the phrasing contrast between a **specific role/duration threshold** (`"minimum of seven (7) years relevant experience as a Program Manager within the Federal Government"`) and a **broad capability claim** (`"managing programs with varying degrees of complexity"`), which the evaluation record explicitly labels as stated `"in general terms"`.

## Grammar & Structure Notes

- **Tags:** `active_human`, `nominalized`, `firm`, `concrete`, `noun_stack`, `penalizes_vagueness`.
- **Nominalized Scope Chains:** Multi-noun sequences (`"development, defense, execution, reporting, and process analysis"`, `"advisory services and support"`) frame high-level staff work.
- **Concrete Duration vs. Broad Scope:** The SOW pairs a numeric floor (`"seven (7) years"`) with a specific role (`"as a Program Manager"`), whereas the quoted narrative pairs `"over 8 years'"` with broad `"varying degrees of complexity"` phrasing.
- **Uptake Pattern:** `penalizes_vagueness` — the evaluation prose explicitly characterizes the vendor's summary assertion as stated `"in general terms"` rather than treating it as self-proving.
